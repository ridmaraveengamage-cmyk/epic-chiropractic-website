Sri Lanka’s original chiropractic clinic

Epic Chiropractic, formerly Elder Chiropractic, has cared for patients in Colombo since May 2014. We are a branch of Elder Chiropractic Clinic in Singapore, Singapore’s first chiropractic clinic, established in 1978.

WHAT IS CHIROPRACTIC?

Non-drug, non-surgical care for your spine and joints

Chiropractic is a leading global health profession that cares for spine and musculoskeletal pain without drugs or surgery. Doctors of chiropractic use hands-on adjustments, soft-tissue work, exercise and advice to help you move well.

Our chiropractors are trained to international standards at accredited institutions, with decades of combined clinical experience.
